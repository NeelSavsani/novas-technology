Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Nova's Technology.

How quickly can our project kickoff?
Following our initial discovery call and requirements alignment, we assign a dedicated team and begin sprint zero within 3 to 5 business days.
Do we sign an NDA before discussing project details?
Yes, absolutely. We execute a mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before any proprietary documents, workflows, or ideas are shared.
Who retains ownership of the code and intellectual property?
You retain 100% ownership. All code, design assets, database schemas, and documentation are transferred to your organization upon project completion with zero recurring royalty fees.
How do milestone payments work?
We follow an equitable, risk-free milestone structure. Payments are released in stages (e.g., UI/UX Approval, Alpha Build, Staging Deployment, Final Launch) only after you test and approve each deliverable.
Do you provide ongoing support after deployment?
Yes. Every project includes 60 days of free post-launch warranty. Beyond that, we provide flexible Monthly SLA Retainers for 24/7 uptime monitoring, security audits, and continuous feature expansion.
